# Task title

Status: active | paused | complete | blocked

## Outcome and acceptance

Describe the observable change and evidence that establishes completion.
State scope boundaries and the user's relevant constraints.

## Question, evidence, and alternatives

For investigative or substantial work, name the concrete flow, symptom, or
decision and what evidence would change the answer. Record the relevant evidence
sources, time window, environment/revision, baseline, and gaps. State testable
hypotheses and disconfirming observations, then the useful alternatives and
chosen approach with concise rationale. Skip this section when it adds no value.

## Context and contracts

Link relevant code, canonical docs, invariant IDs, and decisions. Describe
relationships or language/runtime constraints needed for this change. Separate
facts, assumptions, and open questions.

## Plan and progress

Record the smallest useful milestones and their status. Keep this current
after meaningful progress or before compaction; omit procedural noise.

For authorized delegated work, identify each unit's deliverable, owner, allowed
edits/actions, dependencies, and acceptance check. Keep the integration owner
and unresolved major decisions explicit. Don't create parallel work by default.

## Evidence and decisions

Record checks with command, cwd, tested state, and result. Capture decisions
and short rationale, failed approaches that matter, and remaining limitations.

Where risk warrants it, record the selected review lens or tabletop, supported
findings, residual risk, and the accountable owner or unresolved decision.
Distinguish predictions from executed checks. Link detailed evidence.

## Next action

Give the exact next step and any blocker with the information needed to resolve
it. An external action listed here is pending work, not a permission grant.
